## ZMMR {#zmmr}

**Independent product · German property buying · AI-assisted document understanding**

Buying a property in Germany means reading documents full of technical and legal detail, often under time pressure. I'm building [ZMMR](https://zmmr.ai/) to help buyers make sense of an *Exposé* and the related material, identify missing information, and ask better questions before making a decision.

The early pilot exposed a practical problem: many people discover a listing before they have an Exposé to upload. I'm testing a simpler entry point while developing the deeper analysis. I plan to share the product decisions, limitations, and lessons here as the work progresses.
