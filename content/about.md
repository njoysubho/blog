I'm Sabyasachi, a software engineer and engineering leader based in Berlin. My work sits where platform engineering, developer tools, and AI systems meet.

My work combines hands-on engineering and team leadership. I like understanding how things work below the abstraction, then turning that understanding into something other engineers can use. Today, my focus includes the Java platform and Agentic AI platform at AUTO1 Group.

Outside work, I'm building [ZMMR](https://zmmr.ai/), a product exploring how to make German property information easier for buyers to understand. I use independent projects to learn what happens when architecture meets real user behavior.

### What I care about

- **Useful systems.** A design matters when people can operate, change, and trust it.
- **Technical depth.** I enjoy the path from papers and first principles through experiments to production constraints.
- **Clear writing.** Sharing decisions and tradeoffs makes my own thinking better and helps others build on it.
